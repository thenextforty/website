The Next Forty Methodology

Scaling is not simply doing more.

It is recognizing when the way the business works today is no longer enough to sustain what comes next.

It may be time to hire, delegate, review margins, organize processes, or prepare capacity for more clients. You do not need to start over. You need the structure of your business to reach its next stage.
The methodology in practice

The business never exists separately from the person leading it.

The Next Forty grew from years of working alongside women entrepreneurs. The methodology connects business, entrepreneurship, social transformation, and education because growing a business is not only about knowing what to do. It also requires developing the capacity to make decisions, set boundaries, delegate, lead, and sustain what comes next.

About me

Tania Figueroa

Hi, I’m Tania Figueroa, founder of The Next Forty, a space for conscious leadership and business growth for business owners. For more than twenty years, I have worked at the intersection of business, entrepreneurship, social transformation, and education.

For much of that time, I have worked alongside women , many of them Latina and immigrant entrepreneurs , during moments when their businesses, their work, and the way they lead are changing at the same time.

The Next Forty grew from that journey and, especially, from my work in organizations dedicated to supporting women entrepreneurs, such as Prospera. There I worked directly with Latina immigrant entrepreneurs, coordinating programs and supporting businesses at different stages of growth.

Today I bring that experience to The Next Forty to work directly with business owners who are already growing and need to build the structure and capacity to sustain what comes next.
